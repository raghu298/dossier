Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Dossier install is used only by its owner, Raghunath Mahakud, on his own computer. The only Google account it connects to is his own.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (read): to find replies to job applications and link them to the matching application.
  • Gmail (send): to send job applications and replies when the owner asks it to.
  • Google Calendar: to show interview times and free/busy slots.

Where data goes

Everything is stored in a database on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties, except that the text of application-related emails may be sent to the AI provider the owner configured (Anthropic) to classify replies and draft responses.

Removing access

Access can be removed any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or with Disconnect in Dossier's settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
