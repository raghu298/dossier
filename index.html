Dossier — personal install

This is a private, self-hosted copy of Dossier, an open-source job-search organiser, run by Raghunath Mahakud on his own computer for his own job search. It is not offered to anyone else.

It connects to one Google account (its owner's) to send job applications, read replies to them, and show interview times from Google Calendar.
